A Natural Nest, Made with Purpose

Meet Coir Nest, a nest made from coconut coir that highlights the value of natural materials and sustainable design. A simple idea inspired by nature. Discover Coir Nest

Inspired by Nature, Created with Care

Coir Nest is a product made using coconut coir, a natural fiber obtained from coconut husks. Our product entitiled "The Bird Nest" highlights how locally available natural materials can be transformed into something useful and meaningful. By giving coconut fiber a purpose, Coir Nest encourages appreciation for natural resources and thoughtful product design.
